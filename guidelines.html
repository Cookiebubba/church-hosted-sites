Committee Guidelines

Purpose

A church is a fellowship of people who have accepted Christ as Savior and are united by their common relation to the Holy Spirit. A church, like a community or an individual, is always changing. Fundamental doctrines remain the same, but methods of teaching these doctrines and of moving forward objectives must change if the mission of the church is to be accomplished (Bylaws Introduction).

If used as the guide to follow in carrying out the work of the Church, the New Providence Baptist Church Committee Guidelines help the church preserve democratic congregational procedures in a way that honors and glorifies God.

Bylaw References concerning Committees

I. Membership

Section 3: Responsibilities of Members

3.1 Members shall demonstrate commitment to this body through serving regularly in its various ministries.

3.3 We will adhere to our church covenant (see Constitution, Article 5: Church Covenant and will propose to:

  • Walk together in Christian love (John 13:34-35)
  • Attend our worship services regularly (Hebrews 10:25)
  • Contribute cheerfully, regularly, and sacrificially as God has prospered us (2 Corinthians 9:7; 1 Corinthians 16:1-2).
  • Encourage one another in the blessed hope of Lord’s return (1 Thessalonians 4:16-18).
  • Abstain from all forms of activity which dishonors the Lord Jesus Christ, cause stumbling to a fellow believer or hinder the whining of a soul to Christ (Matthew 18:7).
  • Give Jesus prominence in all things (Colossians 1:18)

III. Ministry Organizations

Section 1. General

1.1 All ministries of the church shall work together to fulfill the church’s overall purpose as led by the church staff and elders.

IV. Ministry Teams

Section 1. General

1.1 Ministry is one of the major functions of the church in which God works through us to carry out His work. Church ministries are carried out through Ministry Teams. The decision to create or disband a ministry team will be communicated to the church. All Ministry Team duties and responsibilities will be listed in a Ministry Team Handbook. The handbook will be updated annually by the church secretary and will list all current Ministry Teams.

Committee Guidelines

Chairman Guideline Expectations

Here are the expectations for those interested in serving as a chairman of a committee:

  • Be a member of New Providence Baptist Church in good standing with the church.
  • You should be a stable/mature believer.
  • Faithfully attend Worship Services and Bible Study (Sunday School).
  • Faithfully tithe.
  • Serve the committee faithfully adhering to all responsibilities listed in the guidelines for your committee.
  • You should not push a committee for your own agenda.
    1. You should honestly present all information to your committee.
    2. You should ensure all decisions are made by a majority vote.
    3. Your vote only counts as one vote.
  • Ensure a secretary is elected from your committee.
  • Ensure minutes are kept for every meeting.
  • Ensure the meeting minutes are sent out by the secretary to the team members of your committee within a reasonable amount of time, but not more than one month from the time of the meeting.
  • Lead your committee in setting 1-2 goals each year.
  • Actively lead your committee to meet the goals set.
  • Present committee updates at the quarterly church conference as is appropriate.
  • Agree to having a background check.
  • You have read through and agree to adhere to the Church Bylaws
  • You are responsible for assuring that responsibilities are performed and fulfilled by committee members and volunteers.
